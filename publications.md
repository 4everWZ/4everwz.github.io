## Preprints & Submitted Manuscripts

<ul class="publication-list">
  <li>
    <p class="item-title">MulRobBench: A Decision-Level Benchmark for Safe and Security-Policy-Compliant Multimodal UAV Agents</p>
    <p>Belal S. Alsinglawi<sup>†</sup>, <strong>Weizheng Wang<sup>†</sup></strong>, Junyi Wu<sup>†</sup>, Lianhai Lin, Mérouane Debbah, Izzat Alsmadi.</p>
    <p>2026 · Submitted to <em>Applied Soft Computing</em>. <a href="https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.23870">arXiv:2607.23870</a></p>
    <p>An offline, protocol-conditioned UAV decision benchmark with 3,024 samples, 17 task-taxonomy nodes, 12 scoring dimensions, and evaluation of 17 multimodal models. Separates semantic decision quality from structural compliance, unsafe actions, and parsing failures.</p>
  </li>
  <li>
    <p class="item-title">H2T-DEIM: Hilbert–Haar Representation with Tri-Modal Multi-Dimensional Pruning for Maritime Small-Object Detection from Uncrewed Aerial Vehicle Imagery</p>
    <p>Junyi Wu<sup>†</sup>, <strong>Weizheng Wang<sup>†</sup></strong>, Liqin Tian, Qing Tian, Yong Niu, Yi Jiang.</p>
    <p>2026 · Submitted to <em>Remote Sensing</em>.</p>
    <p>Combines Hilbert-Mamba context modeling, Haar wavelet downsampling, and structured pruning across CNN, Transformer, and state-space widths for maritime small-object detection and Jetson deployment.</p>
  </li>
  <li>
    <p class="item-title">TRIM: Dual-Budget Structured Pruning with Information-Modulated Saliency for CNN, Transformer, and Mamba Vision Models</p>
    <p><strong>Weizheng Wang<sup>†</sup></strong>, Junyi Wu<sup>†</sup>, Qing Tian, Liqin Tian.</p>
    <p>2026 · Submitted to <em>IEEE ICASSP 2027</em>. <a href="https://github.com/4everWZ/TRIM">Repository</a></p>
    <p>Combines dependency-closed pruning units, information-modulated Taylor/Fisher saliency, coupled MILP allocation, and measured-budget repair to control parameter and computation budgets.</p>
  </li>
  <li>
    <p class="item-title">When Retrieval Is Not Binding: A Diagnostic Benchmark for Visual Episodic Memory</p>
    <p><strong>Weizheng Wang<sup>†</sup></strong>, Junyi Wu<sup>†</sup>, Liqin Tian, Qing Tian.</p>
    <p>2026 · Submitted to <em>IEEE ICASSP 2027</em>. <a href="https://github.com/4everWZ/retrieval-not-binding">Repository</a></p>
    <p>Uses matched memory-off, wrong-entity, and shuffled-binding interventions to distinguish content benefit from episodic binding sensitivity while holding queries, candidates, features, and memory envelopes fixed.</p>
  </li>
  <li>
    <p class="item-title">ManiSeg: A Semantic-Entropy Driven Manifold Collision Framework for High-Dimensional Feature Evaluation of Detectors</p>
    <p>Junyi Wu<sup>†</sup>, Zitong Shen<sup>†</sup>, <strong>Weizheng Wang<sup>†</sup></strong>, Xiaoan Shi, Yi Jiang, Liqin Tian.</p>
    <p>2026 · Submitted to <em>IEEE ICASSP 2027</em>. <a href="https://github.com/Radical0007/ManiSeg">Code</a></p>
    <p>Studies detector feature representations on COCO using semantic-entropy-driven granulation, manifold-collision-based three-way decision, and a composite Manifold-Semantic Fidelity metric.</p>
  </li>
  <li>
    <p class="item-title">EmergUAV-Bench: Protocol-Conditioned Evaluation of Vision-Language-Action Models for Emergency UAV Decisions</p>
    <p>Junyi Wu<sup>†</sup>, <strong>Weizheng Wang<sup>†</sup></strong>, Yi Jiang<sup>†</sup>, Liqin Tian.</p>
    <p>2026 · Submitted to <em>IEEE ICASSP 2027</em>. <a href="https://github.com/Radical0007/EMERGUAV-BENCH">Code</a></p>
    <p>A 1,280-sample benchmark combining RGB, LiDAR, and pose observations with six language conditions, six core emergency tasks, and 16 decision facets; evaluates 13 multimodal models.</p>
  </li>
</ul>
