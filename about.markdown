I am an AI researcher and a **Master of Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning** graduate. From **February to August 2026**, I worked remotely as a **Research Assistant at Zayed University**. My research spans multimodal UAV decision evaluation, visual episodic memory, efficient computer vision, and edge deployment.

My recent work includes MulRobBench and EmergUAV-Bench for protocol-conditioned UAV decisions, TRIM for structured pruning, H2T-DEIM for maritime small-object detection, and diagnostic studies of visual memory and detector features. See [publications]({{ '/publications/' | relative_url }}) for manuscript status and [research experience]({{ '/projects/' | relative_url }}) for projects and software.

Before Adelaide, I completed a **B.Eng. in Computer Science and Technology** at **North China Institute of Science and Technology**, graduating with a GPA of **4.02**. My undergraduate research focused on UAV-based monitoring, object detection, dual-modal perception, and deployment-aware deep learning.

## Research Interests

- Protocol-conditioned evaluation of multimodal UAV agents
- Visual episodic memory and diagnostic benchmarks
- Efficient vision models, structured pruning, and edge inference
- Infrared-visible perception and maritime small-object detection
- Detector feature analysis and vision-language model integration
